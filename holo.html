In the topic of how display technology works, an example can be given, for a display that functions without a screen, are projectors. This device can project an image onto a screen or flat surface using a light source, optics, and other components, resulting in a large scale of display, on any clean or suitable flat surface. Since we need a wall to project the image, there are new methods to create a display using real 3D space to project an image in 3D!

New Era: Volume of Display (VoD)

In order for us to display a virtual scene in mid air, without a screen, wall, or any flat surface, using the volume of space that surrounds us. We can accomplish it, by using a scientific optical method, called “Optical Tweezers” or “Optical Trap.” This method is based on a light source, with a specific set up of lenses, that can trap, or hold the levitating particles, in the light field of the projected area. Particles are in micrometers our eyes can't detect them, and are carbon based, you can use any particle as long as it stands or absorbs the type of light hitting it. The optical trap can hold these tiny particles, where it can be controlled, or moved around using photonics: "LCD" or "SLM" to modify or change the phase of light. Since we’re using real 3D volume of space, we have the freedom of creating real 3D shapes of objects in volume, using the particles. For which, we shine light, to view or render colors, and graphics in mid-air, without the necessity of physical screens.
